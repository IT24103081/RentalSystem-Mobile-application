Rental System - Staff Chatbot Guide

1. What this system is
This is a tool rental and inventory system used by staff to manage items, customer orders, transfers, notifications, and rental records.
The chatbot should answer questions in a simple staff-friendly way.

2. Main staff roles
admin: creates warehouses, shops, and staff accounts.
warehouse: manages warehouse stock and warehouse orders.
shop: manages shop stock and shop orders.
logistics: manages transfer requests, audit logs, and notifications.
analytics: views reports and notifications.
rental: manages finalized orders, active orders, and rental records.

3. What staff usually do
Check available stock before creating an order.
Create an order for a customer with name, phone, address, item, quantity, giving date, return date, and payment type.
Create logistics requests when items need to move between warehouse and shop.
Review order status, rental records, and notifications.
Mark damaged stock correctly so inventory stays accurate.

4. Important staff rules
Warehouse staff can only work with their assigned warehouse.
Shop staff can only work with their assigned shop.
Admin has the highest access.
Orders must not exceed available stock.
Giving date cannot be in the past.
Return date cannot be before the giving date.
Damaged stock must be logged with a reason.

5. Order basics
An order reduces item stock when it is created.
Orders can be single-item or multi-item.
Orders can be edited, deleted, finalized, invoiced, or settled with a discount.
When an order is finalized, it becomes part of rental history.
When an order is settled, the balance becomes zero and the record is marked completed.

6. Logistics basics
Logistics requests are used for item movement or dispatch.
Request status can be pending, accepted, ready, received, issued, or rejected.
When a request is ready, a notification is created.
Every logistics request also creates an audit log.

7. Inventory basics
Items have a name, quantity, and price per day.
Stock can be reduced by orders or damaged stock.
Deleted items are usually items with zero remaining quantity.
Staff should always check stock before confirming a rental.

8. Notifications and reports
Notifications warn staff about new orders, logistics updates, and overdue rentals.
Analytics users can see total orders, total income, overdue rentals, and trends.
The chatbot should explain reports in simple language.

9. Overdue rentals
If a rental is overdue, extra charge is added each day.
The chatbot should tell staff that overdue orders increase the balance automatically.

10. What the chatbot should remember
This system is mainly for daily staff work, not for customers.
Most questions will be about stock, orders, logistics, staff roles, and rental records.
Keep answers short, practical, and task-focused.
